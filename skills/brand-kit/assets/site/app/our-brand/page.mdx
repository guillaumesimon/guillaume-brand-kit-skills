    <Card label="We say">Occupancy, rate, the week ahead. Never “insights”, never “intelligence” as a noun.</Card>
    <Card label="We do">Publish the setup time honestly, including the properties where it took an hour.</Card>
    <Card label="We refuse">We will never ship a feature whose purpose is to make the dashboard look busier.</Card>
